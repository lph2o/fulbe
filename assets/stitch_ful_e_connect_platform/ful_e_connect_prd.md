# Fulɓe Connect - Product Requirements & Screen Plan

## Product Vision
A global community platform for the Fulɓe (Peul) diaspora, combining social networking, professional directory, cultural library, marketplace, job board, and financial management.

## Screen List (Mobile-First)

### 1. Onboarding & Auth
- **Welcome/Onboarding**: High-fidelity branding, language selection (French, Fulfulde, English), and global community value proposition.
- **Login/Sign Up**: Seamless entry into the platform.

### 2. Core Experience (Main Navigation)
- **Home Feed**: Social feed with member posts, community announcements, and events. Location-tagged posts (e.g., Milan, Italy).
- **World Map (Module 1)**: Interactive map showing member clusters globally with country filters.
- **Professional Directory (Module 2)**: LinkedIn-style searchable directory by profession (Devs, Medics, Entrepreneurs, etc.).
- **Marketplace (Module 4)**: Community services and trade (Translation, Web Dev, Transport, Real Estate).
- **User Profile**: Hybrid LinkedIn/Instagram profile with bio, location, skills, and services.

### 3. Specialized Modules
- **Cultural Library (Module 3)**: "Netflix + Notion" style digital library for books, dictionaries, and history.
- **Job Board (Module 5)**: Career opportunities, internships, and freelance missions.
- **Finances & Contributions (Module 6)**: Fintech-style dashboard for subscriptions and community funds.
- **Education & Transmission (Module 7)**: "Duolingo-style" Pulaar language courses and cultural education.

## Design Constraints
- Device: Mobile (Priority)
- Language: French (Main), with Fulfulde/English options.
- Style: Premium Startup / International.
